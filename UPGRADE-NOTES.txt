Local Links v7 – Focused bus and interactive stops
- Click a bus marker to hide other buses; Show all buses restores them.
- Click a stop in the route list to pan to it and open its map popup.
- Click a stop marker on the map to highlight its matching list item.
- Expanded stop name field detection; numbered fallback when names are missing from the upstream API.
- Timetables are shown only when MyBusTimes provides them.
- Requires testing on the Netlify test site; no production deployment has been performed here.
