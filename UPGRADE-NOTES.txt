Local Links v8 — All Stops + Timetable (test build)

This version reads all timetable rows from MyBusTimes service pages, including minor stops hidden when "Timing Points" is selected.
- Direction and scheduled journey selection.
- Date selection based on the dates offered by MyBusTimes.
- Bus type/fleet/registration displayed when available from the vehicle tracking feed (otherwise explicitly marked unavailable).
- Route geometry preserved; clickable stop markers only matched to timetable names when the two stop sequences can be reliably paired.

Important: This is a test build. Live MyBusTimes fetching, schedule variations, route-to-operator mapping, and stop geometry alignment must be tested on Netlify. Scheduled times are NOT live arrival predictions. MyBusTimes is fictional.
